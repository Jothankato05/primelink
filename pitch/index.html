The Problem

Nigeria's crises are cross-sector.
Every response system is single-sector.

When a drought hits a farming community, no single system connects the environmental signal to the financial stress it creates, the health risk it triggers, or the agencies that need to respond. Crises cascade while systems remain silent.

Environment Soil moisture drops below 25% across 847 hectares in Kano North. No early warning is issued because no environmental monitoring system covers this community.
Agriculture Crop yield collapses to 41% of forecast. Farmers do not yet know. Extension workers are not scheduled to visit for two weeks.
Finance 247 households default on micro-loans. Banks flag the accounts. No insurance payout is triggered because no parametric product covers drought at community scale.
Health Food insecurity and financial stress increase malnutrition and stress-related illness. Clinics see the surge but cannot connect it to the drought that started 6 weeks earlier.
IoT No sensor infrastructure exists in the community. The entire cascade was invisible because the data layer was never built.
The Solution

PrimeLink: one platform,
five sectors, zero silos.

PrimeLink builds the data layer that connects environmental, agricultural, health, financial, and IoT signals into a single community intelligence index. When signals converge into a crisis pattern, automated responses execute immediately — no human triage required.

The platform operates at community level — the granularity that matters for Nigeria's 774 LGAs — not at state or national aggregates that mask local crises.

Drought detected → crop failure predicted → loan default risk calculated → parametric insurance disbursed to 847 households in under 30 seconds. Zero claims. Zero delays. Zero human intervention.

Business Model

Three compounding
revenue streams.

PrimeLink operates as a B2B and B2G data infrastructure platform. Each revenue stream benefits from the same underlying data asset — community risk intelligence — creating compounding value as the sensor network grows.

Stream 01
Risk Data API

Monthly subscription for banks, insurers and microfinance institutions consuming community risk scores for credit underwriting, insurance pricing, and loan portfolio management.

Target: CBN-licensed MFBs, AIICO, NEM Insurance, insurtech companies
Stream 02
Insurance Integration

Revenue share on parametric insurance premiums for products that use PrimeLink sensor data as the trigger mechanism. The platform becomes the settlement infrastructure.

Target: Pula, NIRSAL, new insurtech entrants building on PrimeLink data
Stream 03
Government Contracts

Annual contracts with federal and state agencies for early warning, crisis monitoring, and response coordination dashboards. Palantir-model forward-deployed engineering teams.

Target: NEMA, FMoH, FMARD, state emergency management agencies
Data moat: Every sensor reading added to the network improves risk model accuracy, making PrimeLink's intelligence increasingly difficult to replicate. First-mover advantage in the LGA-level community risk data market is significant and durable.
